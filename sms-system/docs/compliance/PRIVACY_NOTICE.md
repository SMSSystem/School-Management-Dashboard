## 5. Who sees your information

**Inside the Service:** your institution's authorised staff (according to their role), the student, linked parents or guardians, and our authorised staff when needed to run or support the Service.

**Exam activity:** the record of an online exam attempt can be seen by the staff who may see that exam's results, and by the student on their own attempt. When the exam is finalized the attempts are deleted and a summary with at most 50 timed entries stays on the student's score; the student, and a parent or guardian linked to them, can read that summary through their own account (the screens do not show it to them). It is not shown to anyone else.

**Outside the Service:** our service providers (section 7); and, where the law requires, a court, the police or a regulator. If our business is ever sold or transferred, your information may pass to the buyer under the same protections. We do **not** sell your information and we do **not** share it for advertising.

## 10. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act you may:

- **See** the information we hold about you, and be told how we use it — we respond within **30 days** (s.6);
- **Get a copy** of the information you gave us, in a portable format (s.6);
- **Correct** information that is wrong, or have it **blocked, erased or destroyed** where the law allows — we decide within **30 days** (s.13);
- **Stop or restrict** our use of your information on certain grounds — we respond within **21 days** (s.11);
- Not be subject to a significant decision made **by automated means alone** (we make none) (s.12);
- **Withdraw consent** at any time (s.9);
- **Complain** to the **Office of the Information Commissioner** (Jamaica) — `oic.gov.jm`.

Parents and guardians can exercise a child's rights (s.5). We may need to confirm your identity first. To make a request, email **[CONFIRM: CONTACT EMAIL]**.

## 11. How we protect your information

We use measures appropriate to the risk (seventh standard, s.30): access limited by **role and institution**; encryption of data in transit and at rest provided by our hosting; abuse protection (Google reCAPTCHA and app checks); session controls and a forced password change when you first sign in; and audit records of changes. No system is ever perfectly secure. Please keep your password private, sign out on shared devices, and tell us at once if you suspect a problem.

If a security breach affects your information, we will tell the **Office of the Information Commissioner within 72 hours** of becoming aware of it (s.21), and we will tell affected people as the law requires. **[CONFIRM: describe security factually only — do not claim "bank-level"/"military-grade"; close the public-default-password and local-storage gaps noted in `LEGAL_REQUIREMENTS_MATRIX.md` row S7 before relying on these statements.]**

## 12. Cookies and browser storage

We do **not** use advertising or tracking cookies. To run the Service we use your browser's storage to keep you signed in for the tab, remember your preferences (such as theme), keep unsaved work on your device until you save it, and speed up pages with short-lived cached data. For staff, the attendance registers also keep encrypted copies on the device so they keep working when the connection drops, and if you step away while offline the screen locks instead of signing you out; to unlock it without the internet, the tab keeps a scrambled, one-way check of your password (never the password itself) until you sign out or close the tab; at schools where it is switched on, a register save that couldn't be sent yet is kept encrypted on the device until it is sent or you discard it, even after you sign out, and only you can open it when you sign in again on that device. After 7 days it stops sending and you are asked whether to send it anyway, export it or discard it; anything left is removed 14 days after it was saved. If you export unsent registers, the file is saved where you choose and is yours to look after. It also speeds up pages with short-lived cached data. A service worker keeps a copy of the app's own code (never your information) so the app can open when the connection drops. **Google reCAPTCHA** processes technical data such as your IP address to protect the site. You can clear your browser storage at any time.
